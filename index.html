Explore Research Publications ￼ About I study how water and ice mass changes interact with the solid Earth and influence geodetic observations. My research combines GNSS, GRACE/GRACE-FO, satellite altimetry, and geophysical modeling to investigate terrestrial water storage, glacier mass change, and regional sea level variations.
