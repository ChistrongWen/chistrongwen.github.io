Scientific notes

The geographic basemap shows land and ocean boundaries only. No GIA, GRACE, or GNSS field is assumed unless you load data. CSV sample points are displayed as colored markers—not interpolated global grids.

NASA/JPL GIA reference ↗
Caron et al. (2018) ↗
Data format: Longitude −180 to 180° (0–360° also accepted), latitude −90 to 90°, value in the units you specify. Blank or malformed rows are skipped. Large datasets are subsampled for browser performance; this visualization does not replace scientific analysis.
