01 · Natural laboratories

Two research settings isolate contrasting loading mechanisms. Patagonia emphasizes unloading of adjacent glaciers and delayed mantle response; the Aral Sea emphasizes unloading of the lake itself. The experiments below use independent conceptual inputs, not calibrated regional predictions.

CASE A · GLACIER UNLOADING

Patagonia Icefields

Ice mass loss drives elastic uplift and a potentially substantial delayed viscoelastic response. Deformation changes the basin capacity of nearby proglacial lakes.

Observation framework: GRACE/GRACE-FO · GNSS · altimetry · RELAX.

CASE B · HYDROLOGICAL UNLOADING

Aral Sea

Loss of lake water removes a surface load, inducing uplift and gravity-field change. Water-storage reconstruction must distinguish water mass loss from solid-Earth signals.

Observation framework: GRACE/GRACE-FO · satellite altimetry · lake extent.

03 · Elastic and viscoelastic response

Step unloading experiment

\[u(t)=u_e+u_v\left(1-e^{-t/\tau}\right),\qquad \tau=\frac{\eta}{\mu}\]

This is a prescribed single-mode step response, not a numerical Maxwell Earth solution. The real response depends on load history, spatial wavelength, self-gravitation and layered rheology. The amplitudes are independent teaching inputs. Transfer uses the current uniform uplift in the geometry experiment.

04 · Time-variable geoid and reference systems

Separate the ellipsoidal height anomaly measured by satellite altimetry from the height relative to an evolving equipotential surface. The geoid response can depart from local crustal motion. At a fixed location, subtracting the same geoid height from water and bed cancels in the water depth difference.

\[\Delta N\simeq\frac{\Delta T}{\gamma},\qquad \Delta H_w=\Delta h_w-\Delta N\]
Do not apply geoid change twice: for co-located water and bed in the same frame, \(\Delta D=(\Delta h_w-\Delta N)-(u_b-\Delta N)\).

05 · Research-ready storage framework

For spatially resolved estimates, integrate the evolving geometric water column, rather than treating every lake as a fixed-area container.

\[V(t)=\iint_{\Omega}\max\left[h_w(\mathbf x,t)-h_b(\mathbf x,t),0\right]\,\mathrm dA\]
\[\Delta V_{\rm corr}\simeq\Delta V_{\rm static}-\iint_{\Omega_0}u_b(\mathbf x)\,\mathrm dA\]
