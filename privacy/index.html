Website Privacy & Analytics

This academic website uses Umami analytics when its tracking configuration is enabled. It helps understand the readership of publications, research notes, and research highlights.

Aggregated information may include page views, visit frequency, approximate country/region/city inferred from IP geolocation, referring website, browser/device type, and estimated visit duration. The geographic information is approximate and does not represent a visitor's precise location.

Umami is designed to operate without analytics cookies. IP addresses may be processed transiently for geolocation and anonymous session calculation, but are not saved as a field in Umami’s standard analytics records. Infrastructure operators may retain separate operational/security logs.

Analytics results are intended for the website owner’s private dashboard, not displayed publicly. Browser privacy tools and tracking blockers may prevent collection. Visit durations are estimates, particularly for single-page visits.

See Umami's privacy policy for details about the service.
